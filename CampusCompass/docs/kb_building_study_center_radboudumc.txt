STUDY CENTER RADBOUDUMC (RADBOUD UNIVERSITY) - WHAT IT IS
Study Center Radboudumc (also called Study Center Medical Sciences) is a study-and-education support location inside the Radboudumc campus zone. It primarily serves Radboudumc programmes (Medicine, Biomedical Sciences, Dentistry-related teaching and clinical education). The building contains study workplaces, education rooms, and support facilities for medical students and staff. It is not a general faculty teaching tower for the wider RU campus, and it does not host fixed study-association board rooms.

MFVN - STUDY ASSOCIATION IN THIS BUILDING
MFVN stands for Medische Faculteits Vereniging Nijmegen (Medical Faculty Study Association Nijmegen). It is the official study association for:
- Medicine (Geneeskunde)
- Biomedical Sciences (Biomedische Wetenschappen / BMW)
- Molecular Mechanisms of Disease (MMD)
at Radboudumc / Radboud University.

MFVN is one of the largest and most active study associations in Nijmegen. It was founded in 1952 and has grown into a broad community with thousands of members and many committees. The association supports students both academically and socially.

What MFVN typically does for students:
- organizes a large calendar of social events, first-year integration activities, and faculty community moments
- runs educational and career-oriented activities (e.g., study support, skills events, talks, networking)
- provides study-related materials (books, instruments, lab/clinical supplies depending on programme)
- represents student interests toward the faculty and helps strengthen the medical student community
- hosts charity and wellbeing initiatives during the year

Where to find MFVN:
- Location: Geert Grooteplein Noord 21 (Radboudumc Study Center zone)
- Route number: 89
- Floor: 1st floor
This is the MFVN association room/board room area. Students can drop by during workdays for questions, materials, or to connect with the board/committees.
