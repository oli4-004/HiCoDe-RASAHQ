CONTEXT ABOUT THE CAMPUS ENVIRONMENT
Radboud University uses building abbreviations extensively. Abbreviations may appear as:
- Standalone building codes (e.g., EOS, HG, MM, ComA, ComB).
- Room codes that begin with a building abbreviation.
Users often see these in timetables and on signs, and may not know the full names yet. Your job is to translate these codes into full names and help them navigate between places.

COMENIUS BUILDINGS A & B (RADBOUD UNIVERSITY) - WHAT THEY ARE
Comenius Building A and Comenius Building B together form the main exam and digital testing complex on campus (???tentamengebouw / examgebouw???). They are dedicated exam- and teaching-focused buildings, heavily used for scheduled digital exams and other academic activities. Outside exam weeks they can host teaching or project rooms, but the primary identity of the complex is exams. No study associations are located here.

COMENIUS A & B - VISUAL APPEARANCE / WHAT TO LOOK FOR
Comenius buildings are low-to-mid, older teaching blocks with corridor-linked wings and a classic campus feel.
Typical visual cues:
- Materials: red/brown brick, glass, concrete; renovated interior finishes in many sections.
- Colors / light: red/brown exterior brick, light interior corridors, some updated door/window frames.
- Shapes / layout: blocky wings connected by corridors; clear stair cores and very explicit A/B wayfinding signs.
- Unique cues: two main entrances; bike racks directly in front of the entrances.

COMENIUS A & B - ENVIRONMENT AROUND THE BUILDINGS
- Greenery: tree-lined lanes, planting pockets between blocks, benches along the paths.
- Bikes: large bike parking directly in front of both entrances; very busy during exam periods.

COMENIUS A & B - FACILITIES / WHAT HAPPENS INSIDE
Core functions and use:
- Primary campus exam location (???tentamengebouw???).
- Digital testing and other scheduled academic activities.
- Extra-time / accommodations exams mainly take place in Comenius A.
- Regular-time digital exams mainly take place in Comenius B.
- Teaching rooms and project rooms outside exam weeks.
- Basement (-1) areas include exam spaces.

Special exam accommodations (important for students with support needs):
- Comenius A is the main site for digital exams with accommodations such as extra time and adjusted testing environments for students with documented needs (e.g., neurodivergence, disabilities, chronic illness, dyslexia or other learning differences).
- Students who need accommodations should contact their study advisor well in advance so the needs are formally registered in Osiris; Comenius exam staff then arrange an appropriate seat and environment.

Exam-day practical rules:
- Always bring a valid ID and your student card. The student card is essential.
- You can enter the exam rooms about 30 minutes before the exam starts.
- You may only leave after the exam has been running for at least 15 minutes.
- Personal belongings go into lockers; lockers open with your student card.
- Locker number corresponds to your seating code.
- Find your seating code in your personal schedule, or scan the QR code at the entrance before the test.
- Login during digital exams is with your student number and Brightspace password.

Elevators and accessibility:
- Both Comenius A and Comenius B have elevators; accessible routes are standard.

Lost items:
- If you lost something in Comenius, you can find the Comenius contact email online or go to the Grotius building for help.

COMENIUS A & B - WHERE TO PARK BIKES OR CARS
If someone is going to Comenius:
- Bikes: best parking is directly in front of Comenius; overflow bike spots are also common below Grotius or near the Aula if there is space.
- Cars: park in the nearby general campus parking areas (often around Grotius/Aula zone) and walk to Comenius.
