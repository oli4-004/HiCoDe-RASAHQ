## CONTEXT ABOUT PUBLIC TRANSPORT TO RADBOUD CAMPUS

Radboud University and Radboudumc are in the Heyendaal district in Nijmegen.
Most students arrive via Nijmegen Centraal station (NS) and then take:

- a local train to Nijmegen Heyendaal, or
- a Breng bus (esp. lines 10, 12, 300 and several city/regional lines).

Always check a live planner (e.g. 9292 or NS) before travelling for exact times.

---

## MAJOR RAIL HUB: NIJMEGEN CENTRAAL STATION (NS)

- What it is: Main railway station for Nijmegen; most long-distance trains arrive here.
- To campus from Nijmegen Centraal:
  - By train: take the all-stations train (Arriva RS11) towards Boxmeer / Venlo / Roermond and get off at Nijmegen Heyendaal (1 stop).
  - By bus: multiple Breng buses go directly to campus and Radboudumc:
    - 10 (Heyendaal Shuttle) – circular campus shuttle.
    - 12 – Druten – Nijmegen CS via campus.
    - 300 (RijnWaalsprinter) – Arnhem – Nijmegen – Heyendaal.
    - 6, 9, 83, 11, 13, 14, 15, 16, SB58 – serve Radboudumc / Universiteit Oostzijde / campus edges.

---

## TRAIN ACCESS DIRECTLY TO CAMPUS: NIJMEGEN HEYENDAAL STATION

- Station: Nijmegen Heyendaal (NMH) – local station next to campus and Radboudumc.
- Train line: all-stops train Nijmegen – Boxmeer/Venlo/Roermond (Arriva RS11) stops here.
- Frequency (typical):
  - Weekdays day-time: up to 4 trains per hour between Boxmeer and Nijmegen, which includes Nijmegen Heyendaal.
  - Evenings and weekends: usually at least 2 trains per hour; some stretches run every 30–60 minutes.
- Walking times from Nijmegen Heyendaal:
  - Science / Huygens / FNWI: ~5–10 minutes.
  - Erasmusgebouw / central campus: ~10 minutes.
  - Radboudumc East entrance: ~10 minutes.

## CAMPUS & RADBOUDUMC BUS STOPS – QUICK OVERVIEW

Below are the main bus stops on or next to campus and the lines that normally stop there.

### STATION HEYENDAAL (BUS + TRAIN)

- Where: directly next to Nijmegen Heyendaal railway station.

## KEY BUS LINES BETWEEN NIJMEGEN CENTRAAL AND CAMPUS

### BUS 10 – "HEYENDAAL SHUTTLE"

- Route: circular line from Nijmegen Centraal via
  Station Heyendaal -> Huygensgebouw -> Erasmusgebouw -> Spinozagebouw/Tandheelkunde -> Radboudumc -> HAN -> Station Heyendaal -> back to Nijmegen Centraal.
- What it is for: fast, high-frequency shuttle specifically for campus and Radboudumc.
- Frequency & operating hours:
  - Weekdays only.
  - According to current data, runs roughly 07:50–22:00, with a listed frequency of about 1–5 minutes throughout the day; during rush hours it is advertised as every ~4 minutes.
  - No service on Saturdays and Sundays.
- When to use:
  - Best option from Nijmegen Centraal -> any campus stop on a weekday, especially during day-time and early evening.

---

### BUS 12 – DRUTEN <-> NIJMEGEN CS VIA CAMPUS

- Route (towards Nijmegen CS): Druten Busstation -> western Nijmegen (Poort Neerbosch, Goffert) -> Spinozagebouw/Tandheelkunde -> Erasmusgebouw -> Huygensgebouw -> Station Heyendaal -> Nijmegen Centraal.
- Campus relevance: serves Spinozagebouw/Tandheelkunde, Erasmusgebouw, Huygensgebouw, Station Heyendaal.
- Frequency & operating hours:
  - Operates weekdays only.
  - Typical schedule: 06:31–18:16, with buses every 15–30 minutes.
  - No evening service after early evening, and no weekend service.
- When to use:
  - Good if you come from Druten / west side of Nijmegen or if bus 10 is very crowded and you only need campus during office hours on weekdays.

---

### BUS 300 – RIJNWAALSPRINTER (ARNHEM <-> NIJMEGEN HEYENDAAL)

- Route: Arnhem Centraal -> Huissen -> Bemmel -> P+R Nijmegen Noord -> Nijmegen Centraal -> Nijmegen Heyendaal (Station Heyendaal, HAN, Radboud UMC, Spinozagebouw/Tandheelkunde, Huygensgebouw).
- Campus relevance: regional fast bus that directly serves several Radboudumc and campus stops.
- Frequency (summary of a complex pattern):
  - Weekday day-time:
    - Full Arnhem–Heyendaal route: about 4 buses per hour.
    - Between Bemmel and Heyendaal in the morning rush: up to 8 buses per hour.
    - Day-time "off-peak": roughly 2–4 buses per hour.
  - Evenings: about 0–2 buses per hour; and the line often no longer runs between Nijmegen Centraal and Nijmegen Heyendaal in late evening.
  - Weekends:
    - Strong service between Arnhem CS, P+R Nijmegen Noord and Nijmegen Centraal (up to 4–8 buses per hour on Saturdays, 2–4 on Sundays),
    - But in evenings and weekends the campus stretch (Nijmegen CS <-> Heyendaal) is generally not operated; you then transfer at Nijmegen CS to another bus (e.g. 6, 9, 83) or take the train to Nijmegen Heyendaal.
- When to use:
  - Ideal if you travel from Arnhem / Huissen / Bemmel / Nijmegen Noord directly to campus during weekdays day-time.

---

### OTHER LINES THAT SERVE RADBOUDUMC AND CAMPUS

From Nijmegen Centraal you also have:

- Bus 6, 9, 83, 300 -> stop "Radboudumc"
  - For the main entrance, west entrance and Dentistry, get off at "Radboudumc".
- Bus 10, 11, 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 300 -> stop "Universiteit Oostzijde"
  - For Radboudumc East entrance and parts of the Science campus, use "Universiteit Oostzijde".
- Bus 14, 15, 83 -> Erasmusgebouw / central campus along with lines 10 and 12.

Exact frequencies for these city and regional lines vary by time of day, but they usually run every 10–20 minutes during the day, with reduced service later in the evening.

## EVENING & WEEKEND SUMMARY

Weekdays (rough pattern):

- Day-time (roughly 08:00–18:00):
  - Bus 10: every 1–5 minutes campus loop.
  - Bus 12: every 15–30 minutes along the same campus stops, until ~18:15.
  - Bus 300: 2–4x per hour from Arnhem region to Nijmegen CS and Heyendaal.
  - Other lines (6, 9, 14, 15, 83, etc.): regular city/regional frequencies (often every 10–20 minutes) to Radboudumc / Erasmus side.

- Evening (after roughly 18:00):
  - Bus 12 stops operating.
  - Bus 10 continues on weekdays until around 22:00.
  - Bus 300 generally runs only up to Nijmegen Centraal, not to Heyendaal; you transfer to 10 or another city bus for campus.
  - City lines like 6, 9, 83 still serve Radboudumc in the evening (reduced frequency).

---

Weekends:

- Bus 10: no service.
- Bus 12: no service.
- Bus 300: still runs between Arnhem and Nijmegen Centraal with good frequency, but normally does not continue to Heyendaal; from Nijmegen Centraal you:
  - take other buses (e.g. 6, 9, 83) to Radboudumc, or
  - take the train to Nijmegen Heyendaal and walk.
- Trains: RS11 continues to stop at Nijmegen Heyendaal on weekends, typically at least every 30 minutes.

## FAST CHOOSING GUIDE

- From Nijmegen Centraal to any campus building on a weekday:
  -> Bus 10 (Heyendaal Shuttle) or train to Nijmegen Heyendaal, then walk.

- From Arnhem / Huissen / Bemmel to campus (weekday day-time):
  -> Bus 300 directly to Heyendaal / Radboudumc / Huygens.

- To Radboudumc (hospital) any day:
  -> From Nijmegen Centraal take 6, 9, 10, 83, or 300 to stop "Radboudumc"; in the evening/weekend, 6, 9, 83 + train to Nijmegen Heyendaal are most reliable.

- On weekends or late evening when campus buses are reduced:
  -> Use the train to Nijmegen Heyendaal plus a short walk, or buses 6 / 9 / 83 to Radboudumc and walk from there.
