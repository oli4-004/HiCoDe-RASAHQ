VILLA OUD HEYENDAEL (RADBOUD UNIVERSITY) ??" WHAT IT IS
Villa Oud Heyendael (also shown in English as ???Villa Old Heyendael???) is a historic villa on the Radboud campus, used mainly for offices, meetings, and smaller academic/staff events. It is the oldest building on campus and functions as a staff-focused venue rather than a teaching hub. The villa houses (and is strongly associated with) the Radboud University staff association / personnel association (Personeelsvereniging Radboud / PV Radboud).
