DE REFTER - ERASMUS LOW-RISE (RADBOUD UNIVERSITY) - WHAT IT IS
De Refter - Erasmus Low-rise
Central campus restaurant/canteen connected to the Erasmusgebouw. It is the main food hall where students and staff eat, meet, study between classes, and grab coffee or quick lunch. It is not tied to one faculty and does not host study associations.
