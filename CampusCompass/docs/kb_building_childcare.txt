CHILDCARE / KINDERDAGVERBLIJF (KINDEROPVANG HEYENDAEL) (RADBOUD UNIVERSITY) - WHAT IT IS
Childcare Heyendael (Kinderopvang Heyendael / Kinderdagverblijf) is the on-campus daycare centre for children from 0 to 4 years old. It primarily serves children of employees of Radboud University and Radboudumc, and is designed as a safe, flexible childcare facility close to where staff work. The centre is a professional childcare organisation with its own administration, staff, and communication, and is not a teaching building or student association hub.

CHILDCARE HEYENDAEL - ACCESS / ENTRY
- There are no fixed public Radboud University opening hours for walk-in access.
- Access is via doorbell/controlled entry because it is a childcare facility.
- The centre communicates schedules and arrangements directly with parents via its own channels.

PUBLIC TRANSPORT / HOW TO GET THERE
- Bus: nearest bus stop is Huygensgebouw. From there it is a short walk into the Toernooiveld/park edge area.
- Train: within walking distance of Nijmegen Heyendaal station.
In practical terms: "get off at Huygensgebouw (bus) or Heyendaal station (train), then walk toward Toernooiveld 15 near Brakkenstein park."

CHILDCARE HEYENDAEL - WHERE TO PARK BIKES OR CARS
- Bikes: park at the small bike racks near the daycare entrance; if full, use nearby racks around Huygens/Toernooiveld and walk over.
- Cars: use campus parking in the Toernooiveld zone, especially Parking P9 (Toernooiveld). Paid parking applies on working days; short stays under ~30 minutes are typically free when leaving promptly.
Because this is a daycare, short pick-up/drop-off stops are common.
