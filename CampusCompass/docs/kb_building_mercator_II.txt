MERCATOR II (RADBOUD UNIVERSITY) - WHAT IT IS
Mercator II is an office and research tower within the Mercator cluster. It is not a student-facing teaching building. The building is primarily used for offices, research, university-industry collaboration, and light R&D/tenant lab activity. No study associations are based here.

MERCATOR II - VISUAL APPEARANCE / WHAT TO LOOK FOR
Mercator II is a slim, modern high-tech tower, visually a sibling to Mercator I.
Typical visual cues:
- Height / massing: tall narrow tower with clean rectangular lines; a clear vertical landmark in the Toernooiveld / science-side skyline.
- Materials: lots of glass, steel, aluminium framing, and opaque spandrel panels.
- Colors / light: mostly glass with dark frames; subtle mirror-like blue/grey reflections depending on weather and time of day.
- Shape / layout: crisp rectangular footprint; glazed lobby at ground level that reads a bit more ???corporate/office??? than ???teaching building.???

From the outside, Mercator II feels like a professional research/office tower rather than a lecture hall: quiet entrances, office-style glazing, and fewer student crowds.

MERCATOR II - ENVIRONMENT AROUND THE BUILDING

MERCATOR II - WHERE TO PARK BIKES OR CARS
- Bikes: use the racks around the tower base and along the Mercator-Huygens paths. Expect less crowding than at EOS/CC, but peaks still happen around office hours.
- Cars: the Mercator zone is mainly bike/PT oriented; practical car parking is typically in the nearby campus garages around the science/Toernooiveld side, then walk a few minutes to the tower. (No dedicated ???student parking lot??? directly at Mercator II.)

MERCATOR II - PUBLIC TRANSPORT
Mercator II is on the science side of campus, a short walk from the main public-transport corridor near Huygens/Toernooiveld. Most students and staff approach via the Huygens side and then follow the foot/bike paths into the Mercator cluster. The campus train stop Station Heyendaal is also close to the Mercator/Huygens zone for arrivals from Nijmegen Central.
