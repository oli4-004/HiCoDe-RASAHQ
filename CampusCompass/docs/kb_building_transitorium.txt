TRANSITORIUM (RADBOUD UNIVERSITY) - WHAT IT IS
Transitorium is a teaching building with mostly classrooms and seminar/tutorial rooms. It is used for scheduled education by various programmes. No study or student associations are based here.

The building sits in the science area on Toernooiveld. It is used heavily for Faculty of Science (FNWI) teaching, and it includes a recording studio with a lightboard for educational videos. Historically, Transitorium is a large-scale renovation with a newer extension, designed as a modern, efficient teaching complex.
