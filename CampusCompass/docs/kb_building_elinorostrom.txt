ELINOR OSTROM BUILDING (RADBOUD UNIVERSITY) - WHAT IT IS
The Elinor Ostrom Building is the main faculty building for the Nijmegen School of Management (NSM). It hosts teaching, case rooms, lecture spaces, study areas, and faculty services for Business Administration, Economics, Political Science, Public Administration, and Geography/Planning/Environment. Several NSM study associations have their board rooms here. The building also connects to and partially houses access to the Radboud Sports Centre, and contains the student-life hub called the "Ondergang" in the basement.

ELINOR OSTROM BUILDING - VISUAL APPEARANCE / WHAT TO LOOK FOR
The Elinor Ostrom Building is a mid-rise modern complex made up of clean rectangular blocks that read as one cohesive academic building.
Typical visual cues:
- Materials: lots of glass, modern facade panels, light metal trims, with brick accents on some parts.
- Colors / light: light and neutral overall, strongly glass-forward; entrances often feel warmer due to lighting and finishes.
- Shapes / layout: crisp lines, rectangular volumes, clear main entrance frontage with signage.
- Unique feel: one complex that serves both NSM (management faculty) and the Sports Centre; large board/meeting rooms and wide corridors give a spacious vibe.

From the outside it looks like a contemporary faculty building with many windows and clearly marked entrances. The sports-centre side tends to look more "active" with people in sportswear coming and going.

ELINOR OSTROM BUILDING - ENVIRONMENT AROUND THE BUILDING
- Greenery: planted lawns and rows of trees that frame the pedestrian approaches.
- Bikes: very large bike storages (often full), extra racks along the main path, plus bike parking underneath the building.
- Traffic / vibe:
  - Busy between lectures, with big flows to and from the nearby Lecture Hall Complex (Collegezalencomplex).
  - The right side is used more by sports-centre visitors, the left side more by management-faculty users.
  - At entrance level you'll notice an underpass-like zone leading to the basement association area ("Ondergang").

ELINOR OSTROM BUILDING - FACILITIES / WHAT HAPPENS INSIDE
Main functions inside EOS:
- Nijmegen School of Management teaching spaces (lecture rooms, seminar rooms, case-teaching rooms)
- Study areas and quiet study cubicles
- Faculty services / service counters
- Large meeting and board rooms
- Radboud Sports Centre access (linked to Gymnasion)
- Basement "Ondergang" with many student organisations and umbrella associations

Food / coffee:
- Blommers (formerly Cafe C) inside EOS: a daytime coffee bar for coffee, pastries, and snacks, run by Blommers Coffee Brewers.

Cultural / event space:
- Theaterzaal C (EOS N 01.260): a professionally equipped theatre hall in EOS. Weekdays until ~15:30 it is generally reserved for education; outside teaching hours it can be reserved for cultural and scientific events such as theatre/music performances, lectures, debates, congresses, and sometimes diploma ceremonies.

Sports:
- The Radboud Sports Centre is accessed via the Gymnasion and is physically connected to EOS; there are shared lifts and internal routes between them.

ELINOR OSTROM BUILDING BASEMENT (-1) - "ONDERGANG" ASSOCIATIONS
The Ondergang is a student life hub underneath EOS with umbrella organisations and many interfaculty/campus-wide student associations.

Umbrella organisations located in the Ondergang:

ELINOR OSTROM BUILDING - WHERE TO PARK BIKES OR CARS
If someone is coming to EOS:
- Bikes:
  - Use the large bike storages and racks around EOS; there is also bike parking underneath the building.
  - Expect these to be crowded at peak lecture times, so overflow racks along the main path are common.
- Cars:
  - Practical nearby parking is typically in the areas around Gymnasion or campus parking zones close to Erasmuslaan/Heyendaalseweg.
  - If you park by Gymnasion, you can walk inside or around to EOS within a couple of minutes.
