HUYGENS BUILDING (RADBOUD UNIVERSITY) - WHAT IT IS
Huygens Building (Huygensgebouw, Heyendaalseweg 135) is the main faculty hub for Natural Sciences at Radboud University (Faculty of Science, FNWI). It is a large science complex with labs, lecture halls, tutorial rooms, study streets, and faculty services for Mathematics, Physics & Astronomy, Chemistry/Molecular Sciences, Biology, and related sciences. The building is also used for practicals, lab work, and science teaching across many programmes. Multiple beta study associations share a board-room cluster here.

HUYGENS - VISUAL APPEARANCE / WHAT TO LOOK FOR
Huygens is a huge, sprawling science complex made up of long connected wings.
Typical visual cues:
- Height / massing: low-to-mid rise with very long horizontal wings; the complex stretches across multiple courtyards, with a few taller stair cores or technical roof volumes.
- Materials: brick, lots of glass bands, concrete, aluminium window frames, metal sunshades, and perforated panels around technical/lab areas.
- Colors / light: reddish/brown brick with grey accents; dark window frames; lots of glass giving a bright, open feel.
- Shapes / layout: rectilinear long wings, several inner courtyards, glazed atria connecting parts, bridging corridors, and multiple recessed entrances with canopies.
- Unique recognizers:
  - extremely long elevations
  - many lab windows/blinds visible
  - FNWI / science signage on several facades
  - science posters and lab-style interior hints
  - an interior ???study street??? with open study alcoves
  - some wings look slightly greenish/wavy from outside
  - you often enter through side entrances because of the many connected wings

From the outside it reads as ???big science campus???: long brick-and-glass wings wrapped around courtyards, clearly more lab/teaching than office-only.

HUYGENS - ENVIRONMENT AROUND THE BUILDING
- Greenery: grass fields and tree-lined lanes along Toernooiveld; planted courtyards with benches; hedges and pollarded trees at the edges.
- Bikes: very large outdoor bike racks and big bike flows at class changes; overflow racks near side wings; some bike parking underneath parts of the building.
- Traffic / vibe: multiple bike paths and pedestrian arteries cross this zone; delivery vans sometimes use lab/service entrances; students often walk/cycle through Huygens to reach Mercator and Gymnasion.
