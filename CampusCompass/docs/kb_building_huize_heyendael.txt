HUIZE HEYENDAEL (RADBOUD UNIVERSITY) - WHAT IT IS
Huize Heyendael (also written as Huize Heyendaal) is a historic villa on the Heyendaal campus used as a representative event and hospitality venue rather than a teaching building. It functions as a faculty club and heritage event centre for Radboud University and Radboudumc, hosting formal university gatherings and smaller cultural or academic events. Typical uses include:
- meetings and workshops
