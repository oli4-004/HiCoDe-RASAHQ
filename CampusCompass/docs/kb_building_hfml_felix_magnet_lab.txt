HFML-FELIX (MAGNET LAB) (RADBOUD UNIVERSITY) - WHAT IT IS
HFML-FELIX is a specialised national research facility on the Radboud campus where scientists use extremely high magnetic fields and free-electron lasers to study materials under extreme conditions. It is not a teaching or study-association building; it functions as a technical lab for researchers, technicians, and international collaborators. The facility hosts the High Field Magnet Laboratory (HFML) and the FELIX free-electron laser labs.

If someone is coming to HFML-FELIX:
