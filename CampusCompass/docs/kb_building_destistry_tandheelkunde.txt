DENTISTRY / TANDHEELKUNDE (RADBOUD UNIVERSITY) - WHAT IT IS
Dentistry / Tandheelkunde is the education and clinical teaching building for Dentistry and Oral Health (Mondzorgkunde) within the Radboudumc medical zone. It combines:
- dental and oral-health education for students
- clinical training spaces where students practice under supervision
- patient-facing dentistry and oral-health care (partly delivered by students as part of their training)

This building sits in the Radboudumc area, which is a "campus within the campus": a large medical complex with its own entrances, routes, and signage. The Dentistry building is a distinct sub-zone within that medical area.

DENTISTRY / TANDHEELKUNDE - VISUAL APPEARANCE / WHAT TO LOOK FOR
Dentistry is a modern clinical-education building, visually connected to the larger Radboudumc complex.
Typical visual cues:
- Materials: lots of glass and light-colored panels, with hospital-style canopies and clean finishes.
- Colors / light: a light grey/white palette with clear medical wayfinding and Radboudumc signage.
- Shapes / layout: rectilinear clinical blocks, wide entrances, and a clearly marked reception area.
- Unique cues: it reads more like a healthcare facility than a classic university lecture building - expect clinic-front doors, waiting-room vibe near entrances, and clear route numbers/medical-style directions.

From the outside, it feels "hospital-adjacent": calm, functional, and clearly part of the medical campus.

DENTISTRY / TANDHEELKUNDE - ENVIRONMENT AROUND THE BUILDING
- This is a busier, medical-zone environment with steady daily foot traffic.
- Traffic / vibe: patients, visitors, students in medical uniforms, taxis, ambulances in the wider area.
- Greenery: landscaped strips and courtyards typical of the Radboudumc campus.
- Bikes: multiple bike parking areas spread around the medical complex and near the Dentistry entrance.

DENTISTRY / TANDHEELKUNDE - FACILITIES / WHAT HAPPENS INSIDE
Inside Dentistry / Tandheelkunde you find:
- dental clinics and treatment rooms
- supervised student clinical training areas
- skills labs / practice rooms
- education rooms used by Dentistry and Oral Health programmes
- reception desk and waiting areas for patients
- supporting staff and administration for Dentistry/Oral Health education

Academic note:
- A portion of patient care here is delivered by students in training, always supervised by qualified dentists/oral-health professionals. This is part of the educational mission and often results in a learning-oriented clinic environment.

DENTISTRY / TANDHEELKUNDE - WHERE TO PARK BIKES OR CARS
If someone is coming to Dentistry / Tandheelkunde:
- Bikes: use the bike parks around Radboudumc and directly near the Dentistry entrance.
- Cars: you can park
  - on the parking area directly opposite the Dentistry building entrance (Philips van Leydenlaan 25), or
  - in the parking garage under/near the Grotius Building, then walk over.
- Accessibility: there are dedicated disabled parking spots right in front of the building.

DENTISTRY / TANDHEELKUNDE - PUBLIC TRANSPORTATION / BUS STOPS
