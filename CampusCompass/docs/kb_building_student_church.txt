STUDENT CHURCH / STUDENTENKERK (RADBOUD UNIVERSITY) - WHAT IT IS
The Student Church (Studentenkerk) is Radboud University???s campus chapel and community/chaplaincy space. It provides a place for reflection, spiritual deepening, and community life. The Studentenkerk organizes both religious and non-religious activities for students and staff of Radboud University and HAN, and offers pastoral support and counseling. It is not a faculty teaching building and does not host study associations. Activities can include services/celebrations, discussion groups, meditation, choirs, meet-ups, and community events. Some rooms are open for quiet use, while others can be reserved for activities.

STUDENT CHURCH - VISUAL APPEARANCE / WHAT TO LOOK FOR
The Studentenkerk is a modest, chapel-scale building with a calm, intimate feel.
Typical visual cues:
- Height / massing: small to mid-scale church/chapel form; not a large tower.
- Materials: brick with glass elements; timber doors/details.
- Colors / light: brick exterior, stained or clear glass, warm interior tones.
- Shapes / layout: simple gabled silhouette, chapel form, and subtle steeple/cross details.
From the outside it reads as a quiet, community-focused chapel rather than an academic block.

STUDENT CHURCH - ENVIRONMENT AROUND THE BUILDING
- Greenery: green borders and a quiet path; small garden-like spaces around the building.
- Bikes: small bike racks nearby.
- Traffic / vibe: usually calm, with community visitors rather than large lecture crowds.

STUDENT CHURCH - FACILITIES / WHAT HAPPENS INSIDE
The Studentenkerk contains and supports:
- a chapel/service space for celebrations, singing, prayer, and remembrance
- community rooms for group activities
- quiet rooms that can be used for counseling, meetings, or reflection
Some spaces can be reserved by students/staff for events, while certain quiet/prayer rooms may be used without reservation.
