CONTEXT ABOUT THE CAMPUS ENVIRONMENT
Radboud University uses building abbreviations extensively. Abbreviations may appear as:
- Standalone building codes (e.g., EOS, HG, MM).
- Room codes that begin with a building abbreviation.
Users often see these in timetables and on signs, and may not know the full names yet. Your job is to translate these codes into full names and help them navigate between places.

GOOD PLACES TO STUDY ON RADBOUD CAMPUS (RU + RADBOUDUMC)

General note:
Radboud University has study spots all over campus. The biggest concentrations are in the libraries, faculty study hubs, and large teaching buildings. Many places are walk-in; some feel "owned" by a faculty but are usually not strictly exclusive. During exam periods it can get crowded, so arriving early helps.

------------------------------------------------------------
UNIVERSITY LIBRARY (UB) - MAIN CENTRAL STUDY HUB
- Where: University Library (Erasmuslaan zone).
- Why it's good: largest number of seats, longest opening hours, clear separation between silent and collaboration zones.
- What you find:
  - silent individual study areas (high focus)
  - group work rooms (often reservable)
  - open study halls / table areas
  - printers/scanners, lending desk, Coffee Corner UB nearby for breaks
- For whom: everyone; safest "I need a guaranteed study place" option.

------------------------------------------------------------
LIBRARY OF SCIENCE (FNWI) - HUYGENS / TOERNOOIVELD ZONE
- Where: Science Library in/near the Huygens Building area (Toernooiveld / science corridor).
- Why it's good: calm science-library vibe, many consistent workspaces close to labs and FNWI lectures.
- What you find:
  - quiet table rows and open workstations
  - generally less "tourist traffic" than UB
- For whom: open to all, but heavily used by science students.

------------------------------------------------------------
LAW LIBRARY - GROTIUS BUILDING
- Where: Library inside the Grotius Building (Law faculty).
- Why it's good: very focused, serious study atmosphere; great for deep work.
- What you find:
  - silent desks
  - study tables close to legal collections and service desks
  - smaller nooks/quiet corners for individual work
- For whom: open study space, but with a strong Law-student tempo and quiet norms.

------------------------------------------------------------
MARIA MONTESSORI BUILDING (MM) - SOCIAL SCIENCES STUDY AREAS
- Where: Maria Montessori Building, main Social Sciences hub.
- Why it's good: multiple floors with open study zones, modern building with lots of daylight.
- What you find:
  - quieter individual study edges along facades
  - open tables and lounge-like seating areas
  - study clusters around staircases and foyers
  - Grand Cafe de Iris inside the building for breaks
- Tip: higher floors and side corridors often have "hidden gem" quiet desks.

------------------------------------------------------------
DE IRIS (IN MM) - STUDY + COFFEE-BREAK MIX
- Where: Grand Cafe de Iris and adjacent lobby study zones in MM.
- Why it's good: good for light/social studying with easy coffee and food nearby.
- What you find:
  - cafe tables (more lively/noisy)
  - calmer study tables just outside the cafe zone
- Tip: for heavy focus, move deeper into MM away from the cafe buzz.

------------------------------------------------------------
ERASMUS BUILDING & ERASMUSLAAN ZONE
- Where: Erasmus Building, De Refter corridor, Erasmusplein.
- Why it's good: many scattered desks perfect for studying between classes.
- What you find:
  - open study tables in halls and corridors
  - quieter floor-level corners
  - close to De Refter and the UB
- Vibe: lively and practical; ideal for "between lectures" work.

------------------------------------------------------------
LECTURE HALL COMPLEX (CC) & THOMAS VAN AQUINOSTRAAT (TVA)
- Where: Collegezalencomplex (CC) and TVA buildings around it.
- Why it's good: lots of seating to prep or wait between large lectures; close to CultuurCafe.
- What you find:
  - open laptop seating in foyers/corridors
  - many students working in small groups
- Downside: less consistently quiet than libraries.

------------------------------------------------------------
ELINOR OSTROM BUILDING (EOS)
- Where: EOS (management/science-side hub).
- Why it's good: big lobby/floor study areas, many students working there for long stretches.
- What you find:
  - open study tables and bench seating
  - window-side desks in foyers
  - Blommers coffee corner inside EOS
- Vibe: productive and busy, especially around lunch.

------------------------------------------------------------
BERCHMANIANUM & OTHER FACULTY "HIDDEN" STUDY HUBS
- Where: Berchmanianum (management/board hub) and other faculty buildings.
- Why it's good: every faculty block has study areas; often quieter because fewer people seek them out.
- What you find:
  - open reading rooms
  - corridor tables near stairs and lounges
  - small faculty-specific study corners
- Tip: explore upper floors and side wings - many spots aren't clearly signposted.

------------------------------------------------------------
RADBOUDUMC STUDY CENTER - MEDICAL CAMPUS STUDY HUB
- Where: Study Center Radboudumc (Geert Grooteplein Noord 21).
- Why it's good: primary study/education hub for Medical Sciences; strong facilities.
- What you find:
  - quiet desks and open study tables
  - education support spaces and rooms
  - medical-student study atmosphere tied to clinical schedules
- For whom: open, but heavily used by Radboudumc programmes.

------------------------------------------------------------
OTHER QUICK STUDY OPTIONS
- Spinoza / older faculty buildings: mostly transitional areas with occasional quiet desks.
- Gymnasion / sports cafes: fine for light reading or laptop prep, not ideal for deep silence.
- Paviljoen / Students' Church zone: sometimes calm nearby seating, but not major study halls.

------------------------------------------------------------
FAST CHOOSING GUIDE
- Need guaranteed quiet focus? → UB silent areas, Library of Science, Law Library.
- Want social or light study? → De Iris/MM lobby, EOS lobby, Erasmus corridors.
- Medical/biomed focus? → Study Center Radboudumc.
- Quick seat between lectures? → CC/TVA, Erasmus Building, MM, EOS.
