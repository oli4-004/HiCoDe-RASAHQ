CONTEXT ABOUT THE CAMPUS ENVIRONMENT
Radboud University uses building abbreviations extensively. Abbeviations may appear as:
- Standalone building codes (e.g., EOS, HG, MM).
- Room codes that begin with a building abbreviation.
Users often see these in timetables and on signs, and may not know the full names yet. Your job is to translate these codes into full names and help them navigate between places.

PARKING GARAGE GYMNASION (P7B) (RADBOUD UNIVERSITY) - WHAT IT IS
Parking Garage Gymnasion (P7b) is the large indoor campus parking facility at Erasmuslaan 0, directly serving the Radboud Sports Centre (Gymnasion) and the science/Toernooiveld side of campus. It is purely a parking space (no academic, teaching, or study-association function). The garage is open 24/7 and is one of the three main indoor parking garages on campus (alongside Grotius and Huygens).

P7B - NAMES / ALIASES PEOPLE MAY USE
Parking Garage Gymnasion is also commonly referred to as:
- P7b
- Gymnasion car park
- Parkeergarage Gymnasion
- Gymnasion parking garage
- "the sports centre garage"
- "Erasmuslaan garage"

P7B - VISUAL APPEARANCE / WHAT TO LOOK FOR
Typical visual cues:
- A modern indoor garage entrance at Erasmuslaan with clear "P7b / Gymnasion" signage.
- Wide vehicle ramps and a sheltered entry/exit lane.
- The garage sits under/next to the sports zone; above it is a sports field with artificial turf (so the roof area looks like a playing field rather than a building top).

P7B - PRACTICAL PARKING DETAILS
- Open: 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
- Paid parking applies on weekdays 07:00-18:00.
- Parking for less than 30 minutes is free of charge.
- Pay at the pay machines inside the garage by debit/credit/contactless card; paying at the barrier is not possible.
- Maximum entrance height: 2.10 m.
- Electric cars: 5 charging points available.
- Scale: approximately 430 parking spaces in a single underground layer.

P7B - WHO USES THIS GARAGE
- Visitors to the Gymnasion / Radboud Sports Centre
- Science-zone visitors (Huygens, Mercator, EOS, Nanolab area)
- Event visitors when parking demand is high elsewhere
- Staff and students who need quick access to the Toernooiveld side

P7B - NEARBY BUILDINGS / "IF YOU PARK HERE, WHAT IS CLOSE?"
From P7b, the closest campus destinations are:

VERY CLOSE (1-5 min walk)
- Gymnasion (Radboud Sports Centre) - the garage is the primary indoor parking for this building.
- Elinor Ostrom Building (EOS) - the EOS/Gymnasion zone is explicitly served by indoor parking here.
- Toernooiveld sports fields / outdoor courts - directly adjacent to the sports zone.

CLOSE (5-10 min walk)
- Huygens Building (HG) - main science faculty complex just across/along the Toernooiveld corridor.
- Linnaeus Building - lecture halls in the science strip between EOS and HG.
- Mercator I / II / III towers - computing science / research towers in the same Toernooiveld science cluster.
- Nanolab / HFML-FELIX - specialised research buildings in the same science-side campus zone.

MODERATE WALK (10-15 min)
- Forum - on the Huygens-Radboudumc edge, still reachable on foot via the science paths.
- Radboudumc edge buildings near Huygens (depending on which entrance you exit from).

(These walking bands are campus-distance estimates based on the P7b location in the Gymnasion-Toernooiveld science zone shown on Radboud campus maps and the garage's stated service area.)

P7B - PUBLIC TRANSPORT LINKS
- Nearest bus corridor: the Huygensgebouw / Toernooiveld stops are the closest high-frequency campus stops; from there it's a short walk to P7b.
- Nearest train station: Nijmegen Heyendaal is the campus station for the science/Toernooiveld side, and connects well to the P7b zone by foot or a quick campus-bus hop.
