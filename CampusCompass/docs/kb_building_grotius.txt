GROTIUS BUILDING (RADBOUD UNIVERSITY) - WHAT IT IS
Grotius Building (Grotiusgebouw) is the main faculty home for Law at Radboud University, located at Montessorilaan 10. This is where most Law teaching, study spaces, faculty services, and legal community life are centered. It is a major lecture and study hub for law students.

From outside, it reads as a sleek contemporary law faculty venue: a long horizontal glass-and-light-panel building with a formal entrance plaza.

GROTIUS - ENVIRONMENT AROUND THE BUILDING
- Greenery: small plazas and tree-lined approaches around the entrance zones.
- Bikes: a very large bike storage area underneath Grotius, plus extra short-stay racks near entrances.

GROTIUS - WHERE TO PARK BIKES OR CARS
If someone is coming to Grotius:
- Bikes: best to use the large bike parking underneath the building; overflow racks are near the entrances.
- Cars: use nearby campus parking zones/garages serving the Comenius-Grotius-Aula area, then walk a short distance to Grotius (this cluster is designed for pedestrian access).
- Bus: Spinozagebouw / Tandheelkunde
