GREENHOUSE COMPLEX AND TEST GARDEN (RADBOUD UNIVERSITY) - WHAT IT IS
The Greenhouse Complex and Test/Experimental Garden is a biology and environmental research-and-teaching support location of the Faculty of Science, operated by the Radboud Institute for Biological and Environmental Sciences (RIBES). It combines a modern greenhouse facility with climate-controlled growth spaces and an outdoor experimental field/garden used for research and teaching on topics like plant growth, climate change effects, pollination by insects, and controlled plant experiments (including GMO-related research under strict conditions). No study associations are based here. The facility is meant for researchers and supervised educational use, not for walk-in student study.

GREENHOUSE COMPLEX - FACILITIES / WHAT HAPPENS INSIDE
The facility includes:
- 8 advanced greenhouse rooms for controlled plant cultivation.
- 6 climate chambers where temperature, light, humidity, and CO2 can be set to specific experimental needs.
- Mesh/netting chambers for insect/pollination or containment experiments.
- A Root Lab (Phytotron) used to study root growth and below-ground plant interaction in large compartmentalised containers without destructive sampling.
- An outdoor experimental field / test garden of about 7,500 m??, including smaller and larger ponds for ecological experiments.
Access is for authorised researchers, staff, and supervised student groups.

PUBLIC TRANSPORT / HOW TO GET THERE
- The site is easily reachable from Nijmegen Central Station by campus buses.
- It is within walking distance of Nijmegen Heyendaal train station.
- Nearest bus stop: Huygens Building (Huygensgebouw). From there, walk into the Toernooiveld science zone toward Toernooiveld 11.

GREENHOUSE COMPLEX - WHERE TO PARK BIKES OR CARS
If someone is coming here:
- Bikes: park at the nearby science-zone bike racks around Toernooiveld/Huygens and walk over.
- Cars: the closest practical parking is Car Park P12 - Toernooiveld. Paid parking applies on weekdays 07:00-18:00; stays under ~30 minutes are typically free. Pay at the machine before leaving.
