Radboud University uses building abbreviations extensively. Abbreviations may appear as:
- Standalone building codes (e.g., EOS, HG, MM).
- Room codes that begin with a building abbreviation.
Users often see these in timetables and on signs, and may not know the full names yet. Your job is to translate these codes into full names and help them navigate between places.

MERCATOR III (RADBOUD UNIVERSITY) - WHAT IT IS
Mercator III is an office and research tower in the Mercator cluster on the science/Toernooiveld side of campus. Like Mercator I and II, it mainly hosts research groups, university-industry collaboration teams, and knowledge-intensive organisations. It is not a student-facing lecture hub and does not contain study association board rooms.

MERCATOR III - VISUAL APPEARANCE / WHAT TO LOOK FOR
Mercator III is a slim, contemporary high-tech tower that matches the Mercator family look.
Typical visual cues:
- Height / massing: tall narrow tower; a clear vertical landmark in the Toernooiveld skyline.
- Materials: lots of glass, steel, and metal panel accents.
- Colors / light: very glass-forward, with deeper-toned frames and changing reflections in sunlight.
- Shapes / layout: clean rectilinear plan; glazed base with a lobby area and seating.

From the outside, it reads as a modern research/office high-rise rather than a teaching building: sleek, quiet entrances, and fewer student flows.

MERCATOR III - ENVIRONMENT AROUND THE BUILDING
- Greenery: green strips and rows of young trees along the approach paths.
- Bikes: bike racks around the base, plus short-stay loops for quick meeting visits.
- Traffic / vibe: office/research atmosphere; visitors typically check in at reception.

MERCATOR III - FACILITIES / WHAT HAPPENS INSIDE
Mercator III contains mainly:
- offices
- research spaces
- collaboration/project rooms
- shared facilities with the other Mercator towers
It supports professional research and innovation work rather than scheduled teaching.

MERCATOR III - WHERE TO PARK BIKES OR CARS
- Bikes: park at the racks right around the tower base or along the Mercator-Huygens paths.
- Cars: the practical nearby parking is in the science/Toernooiveld garages and lots (the Huygens/Toernooiveld parking zone), then walk a few minutes into the Mercator cluster. Direct surface parking at the tower itself is limited.
