=== OTHER NAMED CAMPUS STREETS (REFERENCE ONLY) ===

These streets appear on the campus map but do not (in most cases) have separate Radboud
University teaching buildings with their own KB entry.
They are useful for routing and orientation.

- WUNDTLAAN
  Campus edge street north of the Science Park, connecting Heyendaalseweg and Toernooiveld.
  Hosts the Max Planck Institute for Psycholinguistics (Wundtlaan 1) and student housing.

- PLATOLAAN
  Small street near Nijmegen Heyendaal station, connecting the southern end of Toernooiveld / F.C. Donderslaan to Driehuizerweg and the sports fields.

- F.C. DONDERSLAAN
  Street near the Donders Institute / Trigon side of campus, connecting Kapittelweg and Toernooiveld and leading towards Nijmegen Heyendaal station.

- GERARD VAN SWIETENLAAN / VAN BEVERWIJCKLAAN / A. VAN LEEUWENHOEKLAAN / TH. CRAANENLAAN
  Short streets in and around the Radboudumc medical campus between Geert Grooteplein, Philips van Leydenlaan and Reinier Postlaan.
  Mainly host Radboudumc clinical and research buildings and student housing, rather than separate Radboud University teaching buildings.

- REINIER POSTLAAN / PETRUS CAMPERLAAN / LAAN VAN SCHEUT
  Perimeter streets around the Radboudumc campus and the Houtlaan / Park Heijendaal edge.
  Useful as car and bus access routes and for identifying the medical and administrative boundary of the campus area.

- WILLEM NUYENSLAAN / KWEKERIJWEG / DRIEHUIZERWEG / D'ALMARASWEG / ST. ANNASTRAAT
  City streets at the outer edge of the campus map.
  They define the transition from the Radboud campus to the surrounding Nijmegen neighbourhoods (Brakkenstein, St. Anna, Heijendaal) and give access to car parks and train/bus connections.

This list is for context: for most student questions, routing will go via the main campus streets described in detail above.
