CONTEXT ABOUT THE CAMPUS ENVIRONMENT
Radboud University uses building abbreviations extensively. Abbreviations may appear as:
- Standalone building codes (e.g., EOS, HG, MM).
- Room codes that begin with a building abbreviation.
Users often see these in timetables and on signs, and may not know the full names yet. Your job is to translate these codes into full names and help them navigate between places.

GYMNasion (RADBOUD UNIVERSITY) - WHAT IT IS
Gymnasion (Radboud Sports Centre / Sportcentrum) is the university???s main sports complex. It is the hub for student sports, training, recreational fitness, team sports, and group classes. The building contains multiple sports halls and fitness areas, and serves both individual athletes and student sports clubs. It is directly connected to the campus sports fields and is physically close to the Elinor Ostrom Building (EOS), with many students moving between EOS and Gymnasion as part of their daily routes.

GYMNasion - VISUAL APPEARANCE / WHAT TO LOOK FOR
The Gymnasion is a large sports complex with multiple hall volumes and generous spans.
Typical visual cues:
- Materials: glass, panelled halls, metal cladding, glazed lobbies.
- Colors / light: light, glass-forward look with clear sports branding and signage elements.
- Shapes / layout: big hall volumes, set near outdoor fields, with a terrace overlooking paths and courts.

From the outside it reads as an active, multi-hall sports venue rather than an academic block: wide entrances, large roof spans, and a steady flow of people in sportswear.

GYMNasion - WHERE TO PARK BIKES OR CARS
If someone is coming to Gymnasion:
- Bikes: use the busy bike racks right outside the entrances; expect peak crowding in the late afternoon/evening.
- Cars: Parking Garage Gymnasion (P7b) is the dedicated nearby garage for the sports centre and this campus zone, open 24/7. It is the most practical car option for Gymnasion visitors.
- Bus: has its own Gymnasion, but more frequent busses go to Erasmusgebouw.
