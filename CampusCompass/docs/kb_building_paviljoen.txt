PAVILJOEN (RADBOUD UNIVERSITY) - WHAT IT IS
The Paviljoen is a campus teaching and meeting building on Erasmuslaan 9. It is used for scheduled education, meetings, and university activities. It also houses the University Chaplaincy / Student Church services and the Open Universiteit (Open University). Built in 1967, it???s one of the older, smaller-scale campus buildings compared to the big lecture hubs. There are lecture halls/teaching rooms in this building (often referenced as ELN9 rooms). No study associations are based here.
