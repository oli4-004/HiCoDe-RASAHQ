TRIGON (RADBOUD UNIVERSITY) - WHAT IT IS
Trigon is an academic building used for teaching, meetings, and faculty-related activities depending on programme scheduling. No study or student associations are based here.

In practice, Trigon is closely connected to the Donders Institute for Brain, Cognition and Behaviour. Many Donders research groups and neuroimaging-related teams are housed here. Over time the building shifted from being more lab-heavy to a mixed academic building with offices, experimental/research rooms, teaching spaces, and strong meeting/event facilities. A major renovation modernised the core of the building and added large encounter and colloquium areas.
