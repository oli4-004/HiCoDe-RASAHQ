MARIA MONTESSORI BUILDING (RADBOUD UNIVERSITY) - WHAT IT IS
Maria Montessori Building (Thomas van Aquinostraat 4) is the primary faculty building for Social Sciences. It houses teaching rooms, labs, study areas, and offices for programmes such as Psychology, Pedagogical Sciences, Sociology, Cultural Anthropology and Development Studies, Communication Science, and Artificial Intelligence. Many Social Sciences study associations are based here.

MARIA MONTESSORI - VISUAL APPEARANCE / WHAT TO LOOK FOR
The Maria Montessori Building consists of two modern mid-size buildings connected by a large loopbrug (skybridge). It is designed as a bright, transparent, contemporary academic block.
Typical visual cues:
- Height / massing: two separate modern wings, each mid-rise in scale, visually linked by the skybridge.
- Materials: a lot of glass combined with light/reddish brick, light metal accents, and concrete at the base/plinth areas.
- Colors / light: light overall palette, with very large glass fa??ades; inside you often see a neutral, calm interior palette through the windows.
- Shapes / layout: clean rectilinear wings, big window walls, and the loopbrug as a strong horizontal gesture above ground level.
- Unique impression: it reads as a newer, sustainable faculty building with lots of daylight and a very ???open??? look compared to older campus blocks.

From the outside, you typically notice the two-volume composition and the big glass surfaces; the skybridge helps you confirm you are at MM rather than at the older Spinoza building.

MARIA MONTESSORI - WHERE TO PARK BIKES OR CARS
- Bikes: use the integrated/under-building bike parking or the racks along the main paths. These can be busy at class changes because MM is a high-traffic faculty hub.
- Cars: most students park in the nearby campus garages serving the central/TVA-Erasmusplein area, then walk toward MM via CC/TVA routes. (MM itself has no dedicated surface parking.)
- Bus: Get off at bus stop Erasmusgebouw or Spinozagebouw (both are a short walk to MM). Many campus-bound buses from Centraal stop along this corridor, including the frequent Heyendaal Shuttle.
