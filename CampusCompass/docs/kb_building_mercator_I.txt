MERCATOR I (RADBOUD UNIVERSITY) - WHAT IT IS
Mercator I (Toernooiveld 200-208) is one of the three Mercator towers in the Mercator Technology & Science Park zone. It is primarily an office and research tower hosting the Computing Science department alongside research groups, spin-offs, and knowledge-intensive companies. The building is used for innovation, R&D collaboration, and startup/scale-up activity. It is not a student-facing lecture hub, and no study associations are based here.

MERCATOR I - VISUAL APPEARANCE / WHAT TO LOOK FOR
Mercator I is a slim, modern tower that stands out clearly from the lower campus buildings.
Typical visual cues:
- Height / massing: tall, narrow mid-to-high rise tower; easy to spot from Toernooiveld and the science side of campus.
- Materials: lots of glass with steel/aluminium facade elements and spandrel panels.
- Colors / light: reflective blue-grey glass with darker window frames; the tower can look slightly different depending on sunlight.
- Shapes / layout: crisp rectangular footprint; a clear base/plinth with a setback entrance canopy.

From the outside it reads like a modern tech/office high-rise rather than a lecture building: sleek, glass-forward, and vertical.

MERCATOR I - ENVIRONMENT AROUND THE BUILDING

PUBLIC TRANSPORT / HOW TO GET THERE
- Train: Mercator I is within walking distance of Nijmegen Heyendaal station, which is the campus train stop.
- Bus: the closest bus stop is ???Huygensgebouw.??? From there you walk past/around Huygens into the Mercator tower zone. Buses from Nijmegen Centraal stop here frequently (including the Heyendaal Shuttle line).

MERCATOR I - WHERE TO PARK BIKES OR CARS
