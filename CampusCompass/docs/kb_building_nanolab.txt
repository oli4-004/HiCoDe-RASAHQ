NANOLAB (RADBOUD UNIVERSITY) - WHAT IT IS
Nanolab is a specialized laboratory and research building for advanced materials research and nanoscience. It is primarily research-focused and not a general teaching hub. The building is part of the Faculty of Science (Faculty of Natural Sciences, Mathematics and Computer Science) and hosts high-end research activity, including the Institute for Molecules and Materials (IMM). There are no study associations based here.

NANOLAB - VISUAL APPEARANCE / WHAT TO LOOK FOR
The Nanolab reads as a modern, technical research facility rather than a student lecture block.
Typical visual cues:
- Materials: clean, modern research-building finishes (glass/metal/stone mix), controlled entrances.
- Colors / light: neutral exterior with a high-tech campus feel; functional lighting.
- Shapes / layout: compact research volumes, secure doors, and a ???lab??? atmosphere instead of open classroom fronts.

From the outside, it feels quieter and more controlled than teaching buildings, with fewer large student crowds and more staff/research traffic.

NANOLAB - PUBLIC TRANSPORT / HOW TO GET THERE
- The building is easy to reach from Nijmegen Central Station by bus to campus.
- It is also within walking distance of Nijmegen Heyendaal train station.
- Nearest bus stop: Huygens Building (Huygensgebouw).

NANOLAB - WHERE TO PARK BIKES OR CARS
If someone is coming to Nanolab:
- Bikes: use the nearby Huygens/Toernooiveld bike parkings; plenty of standard racks close to the research zone.
- Cars: the most practical nearby options are the Huygens-side and Toernooiveld parking areas.
  Recommended parking locations:
  - Car park P11 - Huygens
  - Car park P12 - Toernooiveld
General campus rule: paid parking on weekdays between 07:00-18:00; short stays under ~30 minutes are typically free.
