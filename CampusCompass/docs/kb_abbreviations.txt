A

AUA: Algemene Universitaire Aangelegenheden (General University Affairs)

BFR: Bureau Fondsenwerving en relatieontwikkeling (Fundraising and Relationship Development Office)
Radboud Services office for alumni and strategic relations, fundraising and relationship development.

Certificate that proves didactic competence for university teaching staff.

BM: Berchmanianum

BOD: Bachelor Open Dag (Bachelor Open Day)


BSI: Behavioural Science Institute
Research institute of the Faculty of Social Sciences focusing on human behaviour.


C

CC: Collegezalencomplex (Lecture hall venue)
Lecture hall building on campus (Mercatorpad 1).

CFA: Control, functional management and administrations
Finance & Control department responsible for central financial administration, project and ledger administration, control for decentral units and functional management of Oracle E-business.

CIS: Concern Information Systems


CLS: Centre for Language Studies
Research institute dedicated to the study of language and language use.

CPG: Centrum voor Parlementaire Geschiedenis (Centre for Parliamentary History)
Conducts and publishes research on Dutch parliamentary history after World War II, teaches in this field and frequently comments in the media on political developments.

CPO: Centrum voor Postacademisch Juridisch Onderwijs (Centre for Postgraduate Legal Education)
Provides postgraduate legal education as part of Radboud University and has been located on campus since 1979.

CROHO: Centraal Register Opleidingen Hoger Onderwijs (Central Register of Higher Education Study Programmes)
National register containing details of Dutch higher education programmes.

CvB: College van Bestuur (Executive Board)
Statutory governing body of the university, responsible for overall policy for the seven faculties.

C&F: Campus & Facilities
Manages campus-wide facilities and services such as hospitality, sports, culture, lecture halls, large construction projects and building-related services like cleaning.


D

DCC: Donders Centre for Cognition (part of Donders Institute)
Research centre that advances the science of cognition in humans and artificial agents using behavioural, theoretical and computational methods.

DCCN: Donders Centre for Cognitive Neuroimaging (part of Donders Institute)
Research centre that studies brain function using advanced neuroimaging techniques.

DCMN: Donders Community for Medical Neuroscience (part of Donders Institute)
Multidisciplinary research community focused on brain-related diseases and disorders, especially neurodevelopmental, stress-related, neurodegenerative and sensory conditions.

DCN: Donders Centre for Neuroscience (part of Donders Institute)
Aims to understand neural networks underlying perception, movement and cognition.

DI: Donders Institute for Brain, Cognition and Behaviour
Research centre focused on human cognition and behaviour in health and disease.

DMC: Divisie Marketing & Communications (Marketing & Communications Division)
Central marketing and communications department of Radboud University.

DORS: Doorontwikkeling Radboud Services (Further development of Radboud Services)
Change programme to better align Radboud Services support with education and research.

DSZ: Dienst Studentenzaken (Student Affairs)
Service within Radboud Services for student-related support.


E

E: Erasmusgebouw (Erasmus building)
Campus building (Erasmusplein 1).

ELN-zalen: zalen in het Paviljoen gebouw (Rooms in the Pavilion building)
Rooms located in the Pavilion (Erasmuslaan 9).

EOS: Elinor Ostrom gebouw (Elinor Ostrom building)
Campus building (Heyendaalseweg 141).

EXPA: Expertisecentrum Arbeid (Expertise Center Labor)
Develops employment conditions policy, supports staff, provides legal assistance in labour law and advises the Executive Board on its role as employer.


F

FB: Facilitair Bedrijf (Facility Management)
Radboud Services department providing hospitality, catering and related services.

FdL: Faculteit der Letteren (Faculty of Arts)
Faculty offering bachelor’s and master’s programmes in arts, culture, history, language and communication.

FdM: Faculteit der Managementwetenschappen (Nijmegen School of Management - NSM)
Faculty enabling students and organisations to contribute to sustainable societies through research and education.

FdR: Faculteit der Rechtsgeleerdheid (Faculty of Law)
Faculty that has been training lawyers and contributing to the rule of law for over a century.

FFTR: De Faculteit der Filosofie, Theologie en Religiewetenschappen (Faculty of Philosophy, Theology and Religious Studies)
Faculty where students and researchers reflect on major societal challenges and provide philosophical and theological context.

FNWI: Faculteit Natuurwetenschappen, Wiskunde en Informatica (Faculty of Science)
Science faculty of Radboud University.

FMW: Faculteit der Medische Wetenschappen (Faculty of Medical Sciences)
Faculty for education and research in biomedical sciences, medicine and dentistry.

FSG: Facultaire Gezamenlijke Vergadering (Joint Faculty Meeting)
Joint meeting of members of the faculty council and student council.

FSR: Facultaire studentenraad (Faculty student council)
Elected body representing students within a faculty.

FSW: Faculteit Sociale Wetenschappen (Faculty of Social Sciences)
Faculty offering programmes and research in AI, communication, anthropology and development studies, educational sciences, psychology and sociology.

F&C: Finance & Control
Radboud Services division responsible for central financial administration, procurement support and financial control.

F&R: Fondsenwerving en relatieontwikkeling (Fundraising and relationship development)
Team for alumni and strategic relations, and for fundraising and relationship development.


G

Real estate financing model in which the Executive Board owns and leases buildings, faculties and services are tenants, and Campus & Facilities acts as contractor.

GR: Grotiusgebouw (Grotius building)
Campus building (Montessorilaan 10).

GSH: Graduate School for the Humanities
Brings together the graduate programmes of the three Humanities research institutes (CLS, RICH, PTRS).

GSS: Global Staff Services
Central contact point for international staff, offering help with housing, employment conditions, tax, insurance and related matters; part of HR.

GYM/GN: Gymnasion
Campus sports building (Heyendaalseweg 141).


H

HAN: Hogeschool van Arnhem en Nijmegen (Arnhem and Nijmegen University of Applied Sciences)
University of applied sciences with locations in Arnhem and Nijmegen.

HF: Hora Finita
Administrative system used to manage PhD trajectories.

HG: Huygensgebouw (Huygens building)
Campus building of the Faculty of Science (Heyendaalseweg 135).


I

IAD: Interne Audit Dienst (Internal Audit Service)
Independent service supporting the Executive Board and Audit Committee with governance, risk management and control.

ILS: Information & Library Services

IM: Informatie Management (Information Management)
Domain team within Innovation & Projects that steers information provision.

IMM: Institute for Molecules and Materials
Interdisciplinary research institute in chemistry and physics, part of FNWI.

IMR: Institute for Management Research
Research institute of the Nijmegen School of Management.

IO: International Office

IPRO: International Publishers' Rights Organisation

ITS: Instituut voor Toegepaste Sociologie (Institute of Applied Sociology)
Institute that contributes to solving social issues through research, advice and knowledge transfer.

KOF: Kwaliteitsverbetering Ondersteunende Functies (Quality improvement Support Functions)

L

OBP: Overig en beheerspersoneel (Other and management staff)

PS: Professional Services

Bachelor’s programme preparing students to become primary school teachers based on scientific insights.


Q

R

Student collective where students develop creative and professional skills alongside their studies.

RDM: Research Data Management

RHA: Radboud Honours Academy

RI: Radboud Innovation

RICH: Radboud Institute for Culture and History
Research institute studying Europe’s past and present in a changing world.

RIS: Research Information Services

RMa: Radboud Management Academy

RS: Radboud Services

RSC: Radboud Sport & Cultuur (Radboud Sport & Culture)

RvT: Raad van Toezicht (Supervisory Board)

S

SA: Student Affairs
Department offering broad support to students and student organisations in areas such as academic and social development, study success, personal growth and well-being.

SBO: Strategie, Beleid en Ontwikkeling (Strategy, Policy and Development)

SLIM: Student Life and International Mobility

SMT: Sociaal Medisch Overleg (Social Medical Consultation)

SNUF: Stichting Nijmeegs Universiteitsfonds (Nijmegen University Fund Foundation)

SP: Spinozagebouw (Spinoza building)

SteR: Onderzoekscentrum voor Staat & Recht (Research Centre for State & Law)
Research centre for public law, bringing together scholars with broad expertise and strong fundamental, metajuridical and empirical approaches.

SV: (Team) Studievoorlichting ((Team) Study information)
Student team that provides front-office information to prospective students; the English-language team targeting international students is called Study Information.


T

Secondary school teachers who combine teaching with activities at Radboud University to improve alignment between secondary and university education and to introduce students to the teaching profession.

TLC: Teaching and Learning Centre

TSV: Team Studentvoorlichters (Team Student information officers)

TvA: Thomas van Aquinostraat
Campus street (Thomas van Aquinostraat).
