STATION HEYENDAAL (CAMPUS TRAIN STOP) (RADBOUD UNIVERSITY) - WHAT IT IS
Station Heyendaal (officially Nijmegen Heyendaal) is the campus-side train stop on the Maaslijn and the main rail arrival/departure node for Radboud University, Radboudumc, and the surrounding Heyendaal/HAN area. It is not a teaching building and hosts no study associations.

The station has recently undergone a major upgrade (completed around early 2025) to handle very high commuter volumes. The rebuild widened platforms and stairs, added/renewed a footbridge crossing, improved waiting areas, and expanded bicycle facilities. In everyday life this means less crowding, faster flows, and safer crossings in the ???hyperspits??? (peak blocks between trains and lectures).
